Privacy

Last updated 21 November 2026

DOT is a free infinite canvas for organising projects. This page explains what DOT stores and why.

Your account

To use DOT you sign in with Google, with an email and password, or with a username and password. If you use Google, DOT receives your name, email address and profile picture from Google, and uses them only to show who is signed in. DOT never sees your Google password.

Your boards

Your projects, folders, notes and the images and videos you add are saved in your browser and synced to your account so you can open them on any computer. They are stored privately with our hosting provider, Supabase, and only your account can read them.

Shared links

If you choose Share project, a copy of that project is uploaded so anyone with the link can open it. Only share projects you're happy for others to see.

What DOT doesn't do

DOT doesn't sell your data, show ads, or use your boards for anything other than showing them back to you.

Deleting your data

You can delete any project from inside DOT. To delete your whole account and everything in it, write to the support email shown on DOT's Google sign-in screen.
